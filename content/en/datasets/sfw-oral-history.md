The Foundation for Film and Science (SFW) collected audiovisual material with the aim of making it available for education and research. Producing and preserving oral history recordings was one of the SFW's activities. In 1997, the SFW archive merged with the broadcasting archive and the Government Information Service's film archive to form the Netherlands Institute for Sound and Vision.

As part of the *Indisch Erfgoed* (Dutch-East Indies heritage) programme, the SFW Oral History collection has been enriched with detailed descriptions and terms from the *Indisch Erfgoed* Thesaurus. The metadata is available as linked open data under a CC0 license. In collaboration with the platform Onsland.nl, the material has been made accessible to a wider audience.

Read more about this dataset in the B&G blog [Making Dutch-Indies heritage visible: between archive and community](https://www.beeldengeluid.nl/onderzoek/blog/indisch-erfgoed-zichtbaar-maken-tussen-archief-en-gemeenschap) (in Dutch)
