*Wordende Wereld* was a newsreel produced in the Dutch East Indies between 1947 and 1950. It was made by the government film company Multifilm Batavia, commissioned by the Netherlands Government Information Service. The company was directed by the Dutch filmmaker Jan Mol, and Charles Breijer was one of the cameramen. A total of 133 newsreels were released starting in November 1947; while primarily intended for the Dutch market, they were not particularly popular in Dutch cinemas. The newsreels served as a propaganda tool for the Dutch government during the violent period of the "police actions," with the choice of subjects emphasizing economic reconstruction and indigenous culture.
